# Code of Conduct

Quartz should be a welcoming, focused, and technically honest project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information and coordinate security disclosures responsibly.
- Make space for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sustained bad-faith interaction are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Quartz does not yet publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting channel. Do not put confidential conduct details in a public issue; this operational gap is tracked in the project audit and must be closed before community spaces are actively promoted. Once a private channel is listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`, reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as practical. Responses may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents Quartz.
